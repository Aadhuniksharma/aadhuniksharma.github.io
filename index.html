About Me

I’m Aadhunik Sharma — a Quantitative Developer and Financial Engineer with over five years of experience building and deploying high-performance trading algorithms. With a strong foundation in Computer Science and a Master’s in Financial Engineering, I specialize in developing data-driven strategies, optimizing trading systems, and leveraging machine learning to generate consistent, risk-adjusted returns.

As the founder of Quan Algorithms, I’ve led the design of proprietary intraday and arbitrage strategies deployed in live markets, using technologies like Python, C#, C++, and industry tools such as NinjaTrader, MetaTrader, and custom APIs. My work blends the precision of algorithmic trading with the agility of entrepreneurial problem-solving.

Recognized among the top 3% of talents on Upwork and Freelancer.com, I’ve helped hedge funds, prop trading firms, and fintech startups transform their ideas into scalable solutions. Whether it’s building beta-neutral options strategies, market-making bots, or intelligent backtesting frameworks, I bring a deep commitment to performance, reliability, and innovation.
